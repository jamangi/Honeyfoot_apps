# Severity-to-Comfort Experiment — 100 Matches

## Rule under test

- A Care Action with no legal target restores 1 Comfort.
- A targeted Care Action restores Comfort equal to the Severity actually removed.
- Excess reduction does not restore excess Comfort.
- Dr. Honeyfoot is a Supporter and does not restore Comfort through this rule.
- Three Condition slots and two-copy layered stacks remain active.

## Results

| Measure | Layered baseline | Severity-to-Comfort |
|---|---:|---:|
| Archangel wins | 0 | 12 |
| Callus wins | 100 | 88 |
| Average rounds | 8.30 | 9.26 |
| Average final Comfort | 0.00 | 1.92 |
| Average Archangel cards played | 11.99 | 13.21 |
| Average Callus cards played | 7.30 | 8.26 |
| Average unmatched Care Actions | 6.22 | 6.33 |
| Average Condition triggers | 19.27 | 24.32 |
| Average distinct Conditions at end | 2.87 | 2.68 |
| Average final Archangel hand | 1.42 | 1.21 |
| Average final Callus hand | 5.00 | 5.00 |

The rule created the first Archangel victories in the fixed seed set and extended matches by approximately one round. The Callus remains strongly favored. Longer matches naturally produced more total Condition triggers even though slightly fewer distinct Condition stacks remained at the end.
