# Test Opponents — 100 matches each

Every opponent used the same Pressure & Friction deck, fixed seeds 1–100, and unchanged game rules. The Archangel used Everyday Comfort with the Die Stoically policy.

| Opponent | Archangel wins | Callus wins | Unresolved | Average rounds | Average final Comfort | Condition triggers |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Training | 86 | 14 | 0 | 4.87 | 13.76 | 7.12 |
| Pressure | 82 | 18 | 0 | 5.14 | 13.12 | 8.50 |
| Executive | 76 | 24 | 0 | 5.76 | 12.16 | 10.41 |

Training plays the first legal Condition. Pressure prioritizes reinforcing an existing stack and otherwise favors stronger Conditions. Executive also prefers reinforcement, then uses the public Archangel discard pile to emphasize subtypes whose matching Care Actions have already been spent. None of the policies inspect the hidden Archangel hand or deck order.

An initial Executive experiment that simply stopped introducing liabilities after establishing pressure lost 94 of 100 matches. Waiting allowed Archangel to accumulate a generic finishing burst. The final policy retains low-liability intent through selective targeting rather than passivity.

These figures include the corrected Chronic Dampness double trigger and Commercial Hard Floors round bonus.
They also include Toe Cramp's corrected +1 Kinetic vulnerability.
Commercial Hard Floors is persistent and contributes one non-stacking Discomfort during each eligible Care Check.
